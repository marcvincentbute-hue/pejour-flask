Privacy Notice

Pejour Private Personal Journal · Student laboratory project

What we collect and why

We collect your ID number, names, birthdate, calculated age, address, email and username to establish and protect your account, as required by this laboratory. We hash passwords, recovery answers and email verification codes. We record the time of your privacy consent.

Your journal display name personalizes your space. Your start date sets the earliest entry date. Your writing goal selects reflection prompts. Your private intention records why you are journaling. Journal entries contain titles, writing, dates and moods.

How your information is protected

Your private intention, journal titles and entry text are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and are shown only to their owner. Passwords, answers and OTPs are hashed with Argon2id. Administrators can read security event metadata, not other users' journal content. Dates and moods remain readable database metadata.

Security monitoring and email

We record security events with timestamps, typed usernames, IP addresses and browser information to detect attacks. The system sends verification codes and security alerts through Gmail SMTP. It does not send your password or journal writing by email. This local project has no advertising or tracking analytics.

Retention

Test account data is kept through assessment and is scheduled for manual deletion within 30 days after final grading. Security logs are append-only in the application. Final cleanup and any backup deletion are performed by the project owner; this is not an automatic retention service.

Your choices and contact

You may edit and delete your journal entries and request access, correction or removal of personal information, subject to applicable obligations. Registration requires consent under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Contact: {{ privacy_contact }}.

Use fictional journal content during public demonstrations. This educational system is not a medical or emergency service.
